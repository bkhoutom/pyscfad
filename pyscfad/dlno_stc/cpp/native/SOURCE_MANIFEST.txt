  across selected targets with a bounded bare-Gram cache.

  products and their adjoint, and rotates outputs/cotangents back once. The
  original W path remains available internally for numerical validation.
  The pullback releases primal amplitudes after seeding, releases dead adjoint/DF
  caches after propagation, and reuses C for the final DF-gradient rotation.
  The adapter consumes its private DF input with an in-place panel rotation;
  const-input C++ callers retain the copying path.

  propagators; its Frechet pullback retains off-diagonal Fock derivatives and
  uses stable divided differences at repeated eigenvalues.

  construction and explicit seed/domain tau. Original v1/v2 headers identical.
